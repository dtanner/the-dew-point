# Privacy Policy — The Dew Point

_Last updated: July 29, 2026_

The Dew Point is a watchOS app that shows how the weather feels where you are.
It is built to know as little about you as possible.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The Dew Point has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no
tracking of any kind. It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Location

The app asks for location access for one purpose: fetching current weather and
air quality for where you are.

- Your coordinates are sent to **Apple Weather** (via Apple's WeatherKit
  service) to retrieve current conditions. See
  [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- In the United States, your coordinates are also sent to **EPA AirNow**
  (airnow.gov) to retrieve the current Air Quality Index. See the
  [AirNow privacy notice](https://www.airnow.gov/privacy-and-security-notice/).

These requests are made solely to answer "what's the weather here, right now."
Your location is never stored anywhere other than on your own device (a recent
reading is cached locally so complications can update), and it is never sold,
shared, or used for anything else.
